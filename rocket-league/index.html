Controls & mechanics

Keyboard

AD / ←→ drive; push the other way to brake and turn around (hold to ride up walls; you'll drop off the ceiling)

In the air: S/↓ tilts the nose up, W/↑ tilts it down, AD point it left/right

Space jump (hold for height), tap again to double jump

Direction + Space in the air: flip that way (any direction)

Shift boost

QE or double-tap & hold A/D: air roll

Esc pause · M mute · R reset ball (Free Play)

Local Duel

P1: WASD · Space jump · L-Shift boost · QE roll

P2: arrows · Enter or . jump · R-Shift or / boost

Touch

Left thumb: stick. Right: Jump and Boost. Double-tap and hold the stick to air roll.

Gamepad

Stick drives; in the air pull back for nose up · A jump · B or RT boost · LB/X air roll · Start pause

Tips

Boost refills while your wheels touch any surface or the ball. There are no boost pads.

Touch the ball with your wheels in mid-air to get your flip back (flip reset).

Roll one way and flip the other to stall in the air.

Shots: flip into the ball nose-first for a red power shot, double jump with no direction and hit it with your wheels for a purple shot, or flip away and hit it with your tail for a gold shot, the strongest. Flip into the ball with a flip you got back from a flip reset for a white shot.

The clock starts on the first touch. At 0:00 the game ends when the ball lands.
