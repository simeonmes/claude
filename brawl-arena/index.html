How to play

Gem Grab

Gems pop out of the mine in the middle every 7 seconds. Walk over them to pick them up.

When your team holds 10 gems (and more than the enemy), a 15 second countdown starts. Survive it to win.

If you're defeated you drop every gem you carry, so the carrier should play safe.

Brawl Ball

Walk over the ball to pick it up. While you carry it, attack kicks it and super passes it with a lob over walls. You can't shoot while you hold the ball.

Kick or carry it into the enemy goal. First to 2 goals wins. After a goal everyone goes back to their spawn for a new kickoff.

Getting defeated or knocked back makes you drop the ball. If it's tied when the 2:30 clock runs out, overtime starts and the next goal wins.

Keyboard & mouse

WASD or arrows: move

Mouse: aim · Left click: attack

Right click or E: super (when the yellow dial is full)

Space: quick-fire at the nearest enemy · Q: quick super

Esc pause · M mute

Touch & gamepad

Left thumb moves. Drag the red button to aim and let go to attack, or tap it to quick-fire. The yellow button is your super.

Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims, RT attack, LT super, A/B quick-fire.

Tips

You have 3 ammo that reload over time. Hitting enemies charges your super. Stay out of fights for 3 seconds to heal.

Bushes hide you from enemies unless they get close or you attack.
